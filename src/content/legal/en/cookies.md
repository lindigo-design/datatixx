A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work the same way. This page lists everything this site stores on your device.

## Without your consent: only what the site needs

These items are strictly necessary, contain no personal profile and are not shared with anyone. Under French law (CNIL), they do not require consent.

| Name          | Type            | Purpose                                                     | Duration                |
| ------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| `dtx-consent` | Local storage   | Remembers your cookie choice, so we don't ask on every page | 6 months                |
| `dtx-intro`   | Session storage | Shows the opening animation only once per visit             | Until you close the tab |

## With your consent: audience measurement

These cookies are set only if you accept “Analytics”. Until then, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

| Name             | Provider         | Purpose                                 | Duration  |
| ---------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------- |
| `_ga`            | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors, to count visits | 13 months |
| `_ga_PCQVB4JX3R` | Google Analytics | Keeps track of the current visit        | 13 months |

The statistics help us understand which pages are useful. They are not used for advertising, and Google's advertising features are switched off. More details in our [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

The site does not currently use preference or marketing cookies. If that changes, we will update this page and ask for your consent again.

## Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time via **“Cookies”** at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your consent, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted from your browser.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works fully without them.
